## Review Instructions

Systematically evaluate the trigger extraction against the source conversation across four dimensions:

### 1. Grounding Check
For each extracted trigger, verify it is either:
- **Explicitly stated** — the exact term or a clear synonym appears in the conversation
- **Strongly implied** — the clinical context makes it unambiguous without being stated directly
Flag anything that is inferred, assumed, or fabricated.

### 2. Hallucination Detection
Identify triggers that are claimed in the extraction but have NO basis in the conversation. Note the specific trigger and why it is unsupported.

### 3. Omission Detection
Identify triggers that are clearly present in the conversation but missing from the extraction. Note the exact phrase or passage and what trigger type it should have been assigned.

### 4. Classification Accuracy
For each trigger, verify the `trigger_type` label is clinically appropriate. Flag misclassifications and suggest the correct label where applicable.

### 5. Confidence Score
Assign a score from 0–10 reflecting overall extraction quality:
- 9–10: Near-perfect, no meaningful errors
- 7–8: Minor omissions or edge-case misclassifications
- 4–6: Several errors that affect reliability
- 1–3: Significant hallucinations or omissions
- 0: Extraction is unusable
