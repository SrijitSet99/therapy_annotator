Counselor: Hi, thanks for coming in today. How are you feeling about your smoking?

Patient: Honestly, I've been thinking about it a lot lately. My doctor told me last month that my lungs are showing early signs of damage. That scared me.

Counselor: That sounds like it was a real wake-up call. Have you thought about quitting before?

Patient: Oh yeah, I've tried a few times. Last year I used the nicotine patch for about six weeks — that was my longest stretch. Before that I tried just stopping cold turkey but that only lasted maybe two or three days.

Counselor: Six weeks is actually a meaningful attempt. What happened at the end of those six weeks?

Patient: Work stress. It was a brutal project and I just cracked one night. Lit up and then it was back to normal within a week.

Counselor: So stress is a big trigger for you?

Patient: Definitely. And mornings — I have a cigarette with my coffee every single morning. It's the first thing I do. I don't even feel awake without it.

Counselor: Those habitual morning smokes can be really hard to break. What worries you most about quitting this time?

Patient: Honestly? The stress thing. I don't know what I'd do when things get hard at work. Also, my wife quit two years ago and she gained quite a bit of weight. That freaks me out a little.

Counselor: Those are both very common concerns. Let me tell you what we can do. First, I'd really recommend we talk about varenicline — it's a prescription medication that reduces cravings and has been shown to be more effective than the patch alone. Second, we should build you a stress-management plan so you have something to reach for other than a cigarette. That might include breathing techniques, short walks, or even just having a glass of water. Third, the weight concern is real but manageable — a nutritionist referral alongside your quit attempt can help a lot. Does a quit date in the next two to three weeks feel doable?

Patient: Maybe. I'd like to at least set a date. I think I'm ready to actually try this time, not just think about it.

Counselor: That's a great sign. Let's schedule you for varenicline and set that date today.
